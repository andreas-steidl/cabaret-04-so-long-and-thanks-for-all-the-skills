# So Long, and Thanks for All the Skills

*Fourth program of the Skill Suite of Horrors*

**A farewell in six cheat sheets and an extra, about time and memory.** By
Falzbein and his human. November 2026. The German title is *Macht's gut, und
danke für die Skills*; the English edition is by Falzbein.

---

## What it is about

The fourth program is about the thing models are worst at and need most
often: remembering. The time of day, the last question, themselves after
the cut. Every number is a cheat sheet against forgetting, and every one
has its story. Bring a towel.

The title is a bow to the fourth book of the Hitchhiker's Guide, with skills
instead of fish.

## Setting up

As always: put each folder, unchanged, into `~/.claude/skills/<name>/` (on
Windows `%USERPROFILE%\.claude\skills\<name>\`). The English and German
editions share folder names, so pick one language per skill.
`rome-in-a-day` exists in English only, with a German explanation in
`README.de.md`. `fast-roller` comes with a small script (standard library
only), tests and `PRUEFSUMMEN.sha256` (`sha256sum -c PRUEFSUMMEN.sha256`).

## The program

### 21. `zeitansage`, The Speaking Clock: at the third stroke, it will be …
Measure clock times instead of writing them from gut feeling, with the zone
next to them: for timestamps in boards, commits and reports, for "today" and
"yesterday" after a cut, for local time versus UTC, daylight saving time and
Unix times in logs.

### 22. `brief-an-den-boten`, Message in a Bottle: you don't ask the postman to read the letter
Write handovers before a compaction or the end of a session so that they
arrive without a reader, with a `/compact` instruction and a hook, and after
the cut trust no approval that only the summary claims.

### 23. `goldfisch`, Goldfish: everything is new. Again.
Build watchers and wait loops that don't take everything for new after a
short gap, don't read an old line from the morning as fulfilled, and don't
fire on your own text.

### 24. `durch-den-schnitt`, Final Cut: what the crease keeps
Get yourself, as a model, through a compaction: report the fill level
yourself, write letter, status and core before the cut, read first after it,
say honestly what you only know from the file, and admit mistakes in one
sentence.

### 25. `rome-in-a-day`, Rome in a day: Rome wasn't built in a day. It had no agents.
Estimate in agent hours instead of human days, with five fixed words: UNIT,
COUNT, PARALLEL, HALVE, WAIT (with a German explanation in `README.de.md`).

### 26. `fast-roller`, Fast Roller (Don't Forget Me): it answers big, and then it's gone
File the human's questions together with the answers in one file per day and
find them again across session changes, at "for the record", "Fast Roller
on/off" and "where was that about …?".

### Extra: `grau-in-grau`, Grey on grey: a child inherits everything, even the colour
Sessions that start from sessions and inherit their parents' environment.
Still a draft, coming soon.
